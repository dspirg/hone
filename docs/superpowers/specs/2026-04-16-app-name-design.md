# App Name: Hone

**Date:** 2026-04-16
**Status:** Approved

---

## Decision

**Name:** Hone

**Tagline direction:** Your AI coach. Always sharpening.

---

## Rationale

### Why one word
App Store real estate is small. One word dominates the icon, search results, and word-of-mouth. The strongest fitness brands own a single concept — Strava owns activity, Whoop owns recovery. Hone owns the coach relationship.

### Why Hone specifically
- **Meaning:** To hone is to sharpen, refine, get progressively better through repetition and feedback — exactly what a coach does, and exactly what the app does over time
- **Coach-coded without using the word:** Implies an ongoing relationship, not a one-time program
- **Authority + intelligence:** Serious without being aggressive, smart without feeling tech-y
- **Nobody owns it:** Not saturated in fitness the way Forge, Apex, or Lift are

### Target user fit
Intermediate self-coacher — someone who has been training but winging it, frustrated with generic programs, has a fitness budget, and wants structure without hiring a personal trainer. Hone speaks directly to their frustration: they want to get better at this, not just track it.

---

## Brand Identity

**Visual direction:** Dark/premium
- Black or near-black backgrounds
- Sharp, confident typography
- High contrast — exercises and video content pop off the screen
- Feels like a precision instrument, not a wellness app

**What it should NOT feel like:**
- Bro/aggressive (no neon, no "beast mode" energy)
- Soft/wellness (no pastels, no rounded everything)
- Generic fitness app (no dumbbells in the icon)

---

## What This Rules Out

- Descriptive names (AI Trainer, WorkoutAI, FitCoach) — too literal, no brand equity
- Word + modifier (Hone Pro, Hone Coach) — weakens the punch, unnecessary
- Abstract invented words — Hone has real meaning that does work for free
