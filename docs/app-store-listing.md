## Description

```
Meet Hone, the coach who knows you.

Hone builds workout plans around your goals, your equipment, and your schedule. No generic programs. No guesswork. Just a training plan that fits your life and evolves as you improve.

Tell Hone what you have to work with -- a full gym, a pair of dumbbells, or just your bodyweight -- and get a complete training program in seconds. Every exercise comes with guided video demonstrations so you always know exactly what to do.

During your sessions, Hone tracks every set and rep, celebrates your personal records, and adjusts your plan based on how you perform. Struggled with today's workout? Hone scales it back. Crushing it? Hone pushes you further.

Between sessions, chat with Hone about your training, ask for exercise swaps, or get advice on your program. It's like having a personal trainer available whenever you need one.

Features:
- AI-generated workout plans personalized to your goals and equipment
- Guided exercise videos for every movement
- Real-time session tracking with set and rep logging
- Adaptive training that evolves based on your performance
- Personal records and progress tracking
- Conversational AI coach available anytime

Your next workout is ready. Let's go.
```
