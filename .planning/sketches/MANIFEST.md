# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
Dark mode with warm amber/orange accents. Premium gym at night — dark walls, warm lighting, clean equipment. Confident but welcoming. The AI coach should feel human and approachable, not clinical. Named "Hone" with branded identity.

## Reference Points
Fitbod (dark premium), Hevy (modern friendly), Strong (speed-first logging). Target: Fitbod's premium feel + warmer, more personal coach presence.
