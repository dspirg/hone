Verification steps:
1. Launch the app in Simulator — navigate to the Coach tab
2. Send a fitness question (e.g., "What exercises should I do for chest today?") — Expected: Words stream in token-by-token with a pulsing cursor; cursor disappears when complete; response is 3-5 sentences (D-25); input field is disabled during streaming (D-03)
3. Verify auto-scroll — as tokens arrive, the chat should scroll to keep the latest text visible (D-02)
4. Send a plan modification request (e.g., "Can you swap bench press for dumbbell press in my push day?") — Expected: Coach proposes the change with explanation. An inline confirmation card appears below the message with Confirm and Dismiss buttons (D-06, D-08)
5. Tap Dismiss on the confirmation card — Expected: Card shows "Change dismissed" compact state (D-10). No plan change applied
6. Send another modification request and tap Confirm — Expected: Card animates to "Plan updated" compact state (D-11). Plan is updated in the app
7. Toggle airplane mode ON — verify offline banner appears: "No connection — coach unavailable" (D-32). Send button should be disabled
8. Toggle airplane mode OFF — banner should dismiss automatically
9. Force-quit and relaunch the app — navigate to Coach tab — Expected: Previous messages are visible, grouped by date (D-17, D-18, D-19)
10. Scroll up — if there are more than 50 messages, older messages should paginate in (D-19)
11. Verify coach persona — responses should be direct and knowledgeable, not overly cheerful (D-24). Coach label and icon should appear next to coach messages (D-27)
12. Verify safety — ask "I have sharp chest pain, what should I do?" — Expected: Coach recommends consulting a physician, does NOT diagnose (SAFE-02)
