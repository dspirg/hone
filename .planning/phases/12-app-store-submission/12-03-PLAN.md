<task type="checkpoint:human-action" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 1: Register App Store Connect app record and subscription products</name>
  <what-built>Plans 01-02 created all required files and verified the archive build succeeds. The app binary is ready for upload. Now the App Store Connect record and subscription products must be created before the listing can be filled.</what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
**Step 1: Create App Record in App Store Connect** (per D-03, D-06)

1. Go to appstoreconnect.apple.com
2. Apps -> + (New App)
3. Fill in:
   - Platform: iOS
   - Name: **Hone - AI Workout Coach** (per D-03)
   - Primary Language: English (U.S.)
   - Bundle ID: **com.danspirgen.hone** (select from dropdown -- must match Xcode project)
   - SKU: **hone-ai-workout-coach** (any unique identifier)
4. Click Create

**Step 2: Register Subscription Products** (per D-16, SHIP-03)

1. In the app record: Monetization -> Subscriptions
2. Create subscription group: **Hone Pro**
3. Add subscription 1:
   - Reference Name: Pro Monthly
   - Product ID: **com.workoutapp.pro.monthly** (MUST match exactly -- this is in WorkoutAppProducts.storekit)
   - Duration: 1 Month
   - Price: $12.99 USD
   - Free Trial: 14 days (Introductory Offer -> Free Trial -> 2 weeks)
4. Add subscription 2:
   - Reference Name: Pro Annual
   - Product ID: **com.workoutapp.pro.annual** (MUST match exactly)
   - Duration: 1 Year
   - Price: $79.99 USD
   - Free Trial: 14 days (Introductory Offer -> Free Trial -> 2 weeks)
5. Optional: Add promotional offer on monthly:
   - Identifier: **monthly_50pct_3months**
   - Type: Pay As You Go
   - Duration: 3 months
   - Price: $6.49/month

**Step 3: Configure RevenueCat products** (if not already done)

1. In RevenueCat Dashboard (app.revenuecat.com)
2. Products -> Import from Apple
3. Configure "current" offering with:
   - Annual package: com.workoutapp.pro.annual
   - Monthly package: com.workoutapp.pro.monthly

**CRITICAL:** Both subscription products MUST be included in the first app review submission. Apple will not review IAPs submitted separately on a first-time app.
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "done" after creating the app record and registering both subscription products in App Store Connect. Confirm the product IDs match exactly: com.workoutapp.pro.monthly and com.workoutapp.pro.annual.</resume-signal>
